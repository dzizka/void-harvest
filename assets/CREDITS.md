# Assets

All 3D models are CC0 (public domain); attribution is not required but appreciated.

- **Kenney – Space Kit** (kenney.nl) – `models/kenney/` (miner ship, asteroids)
- **Quaternius – LowPoly Spaceships** (quaternius.com) – `models/quaternius/` (player ships)
- **Majadroid – 3D LowPoly Spaceships and Components** (majadroid.itch.io) – `models/majadroid/` (enemies)

`sprites/*.webp` are rendered from these models by `tools/render3d/sheet.js` (settings in `tools/render3d/specs.json`).
