Overview

Void Harvest is a 2D space shooter with Diablo-style progression. You fly one of four ships, mine asteroids, fight across six sectors, collect items with rarities and affixes, build your ship from talents and beat bosses in gates.

First steps

  1. Pick a ship in the hangar. Each has its own skills, dodge and talent tree.
  2. Follow the tutorial: mining, combat, talent, gear, station, upgrade, gate.
  3. The blue circle around the beacon is a safe zone. The further from the beacon, the stronger the enemies (Depth I–III).
  4. Gates (G on the minimap) lead to bosses. Bosses drop Nightmare Gate keys and some of them unlock a higher world tier.

The game also supports touch (phone in landscape) and gamepads.

Key rules

  • The level cap is 50; Paragon continues after that.
  • Dying costs 15 % of the ore you carry; the ship is repaired at Haven Station.
  • The game saves every 15 s, when a window closes and when you leave the page. Progress lives in the browser; back it up to a file (also to move it PC ↔ phone) in the hangar or Menu → Export / Import backup.
  • Stash, materials, home base, achievements and the season are shared by all ships.
  • Systems unlock gradually: Workshop 5, Base 8, Season 10, Challenges 12 (by your best account level).

Skill points

1 point every 5 levels (up to 10). Rank 1: −15 % cooldown and +25 % skill damage. Rank 2: pick one of two modifiers. The dodge has its own modifiers: Double Dodge – +1 dodge charge. · Shock Dodge – Dodging leaves an explosion for 150 % laser damage.

Talents and builds

Each ship has 3 talent branches. You get 1 point per level. The branch's second tier unlocks after 5 points in it, the third after 10, the key talent after 15 (only one per ship). Ranks from gear can go at most +3 above the maximum. Talent reset is free up to level 10.

Each branch is also a build: it has its own 4-piece set, a “+2 to branch talents” legendary and recommended affixes the game uses for item advice.

Item level (iLvl)

iLvl = the level of the enemy that dropped it (your level + world tier + depth). Each iLvl adds +5 % to affixes (area damage and mining yield +1.2 %).

Ancestral, greater affixes, primal

An Ancestral item (gold corner) has every value +20 %. A greater affix ✦ is worth ×1.5. A Primal item is Ancestral with every roll at maximum. Mythics are always Ancestral, maxed and carry 1 greater affix.

Upgrading

Inventory → click an equipped slot: +1 to +5 for ore (+10 % base values per step). The Workshop tempers further to +10 (ore and shards) and Legendaries, sets and Mythics up to +15; steps 12 and 15 empower a random affix ×1.25. Prices rise for items above iLvl 20.

Workshop

Reforge: reroll a value or swap an affix for another (each one costs more). Sockets for gems. Imprint: puts a legendary power from your codex onto a rare item. The stash is shared by all ships (120 slots).

Endgame

Nightmare Gates

A key turns any gate into a Nightmare Gate. Limit 4:00 – in time you get a key one level higher, within half the limit two. Dying loses the key. Each key has 1–3 threats and 1 bonus. You can hold up to 20 keys per ship (more turn into 40 ore); scrap a key you don't need in the gate window for 40 + 10 × level ore.

Threats

  • Unstable Hulls – Enemies explode shortly after dying.

Station modes

Architect's Rift · defeated

The ultimate boss. You open it with shards from four bosses (World II: 40 %, World III and IV and Nightmare Gates 10+: always). Reward: Mythic Crown, rune, set piece, World IV.

Void Climb · record floor

5:00 to reach the highest floor possible. 40 points per floor (elite 6), every 5th floor is guarded by the Warden of the Deep. Enemies get +17 % health and +6 % damage per floor; World Tier does not apply. Rewards: Shards, gems, Legendaries, rune upgrades, Set items from floor 15, Mythics from floor 20. From level 50.

Smugglers' Vault

Map fragments drop from elites (8 %, from level 15). 5 fragments open the vault: 60 s, 26 Chests and Smugglers who run away. A Smuggler carries a Legendary, a gem, Shards and, from World II, a chance for a Set item.

Commanders' Arena

All 6 sector bosses back to back against the clock, always at fixed World IV difficulty and 5× health. Reward: 30 Shards, 2 Legendaries from the boss loot tables, 60 % Set, 50 % rune. From level 50.

Void Horde

6 waves of 60 s. After each wave you pick a boon – some come with a curse that gives more aether. Aether drops from slain enemies (elites 5). At the end, the horde lord and hoards you open with aether. From level 30, difficulty follows the world tier.

Weekly Challenge

A Void Climb from floor 1 with two modifiers that change every Monday. First time reaching floor 10 each week (per ship): 50 Shards and a Set item.

Void Horde

Boons: +25 % damage · +30 % fire rate · −25 % skill cooldowns · +15 % Critical Chance · +50 % aether · Every kill restores 1 % Hull

Banes: Enemies +30 % health · Enemies +25 % damage · Enemies +20 % speed · More elites · Enemies explode on death

Treasures: Armory (40) – 3 items, 1 legendary · Treasury (30) – 2 gems and shards · Stash (25) – Ore, shards, plasma and dark matter · Horde Lord's Hoard (120) – 2 legendaries, chance of a set and a mythic

Void Storm

From level 20 it appears every 15 min for 10 min in some sector. More and stronger enemies, void embers and cursed chests (60 embers) and a gift (175).

Strongholds

Every combat sector has an occupied stronghold (from level 5). Get close and beat 3 waves and a commander. A freed stronghold becomes an outpost with a safe zone.

World boss

The Star Devourer appears every 20 min (announced in advance) and stays 5 min. Drops 2 Legendaries, 3 items, 2 gems, 15 shards, a key, from tier II a 50 % set, a 30 % rune and a Mythic chance.

Paragon and runes

After level 50 you earn Paragon points in four constellations. Each has 14 points: most are small bonuses, the 5th and 10th are medium and the 14th is a star (big bonus). Socket a star rune into a constellation; its bonus turns on after 10 points there. Runes level up (max 30) by finishing a Nightmare Gate at least as high as the rune.
